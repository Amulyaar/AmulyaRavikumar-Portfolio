01 / Finding

Rank 1 earned 2.63× the observed CTR of ranks 2–10.

Across the analyzed sample, the first position received about 10% of impressions and 22.6% of clicks. That pattern makes placement a key confounder when teams interpret raw click-through changes.

03 / Product readout

Don't credit a ranking change for a placement advantage.

Interpretation: rank 1's 2.63× CTR ratio is consistent with position bias. It may also reflect differences in item quality, audience, or slate context. This sample cannot isolate those factors.

What I would change in measurement

  • Log full candidate slates, positions, ranker version, and exposure propensities.
  • Use position-aware diagnostics before comparing click outcomes.
  • Pair engagement with completion, hides/reports, repeat exposure, and content-mix guardrails.

What I would test next

  • Randomize eligible slates between control and candidate rankers.
  • Keep the candidate pool constant so the ranking policy is the main difference.
  • Predefine quality and ecosystem guardrails; inspect outcomes by creator, category, and format where labels exist.
04 / Proposed experiment · not run

Test the position effect while holding the candidate item constant.

The audit motivates a test; it does not provide a causal effect. Randomize users consistently to prevent repeated slates from crossing arms.

Test design

  • Control: original ranking.
  • Treatment: swap rank 1 with one uniformly selected item from ranks 2–10; retain the same candidate set.
  • Primary: CTR of the preselected moved item within a prespecified attribution window.
  • Analysis: intention-to-treat with user-clustered uncertainty.

Guardrails to instrument

  • Useful engagement/read depth.
  • Hides, reports, or quick returns where available.
  • Exposure concentration and category/creator/format coverage where labels exist.

05 / Method & limits

A bounded, reproducible real-data audit.

  1. Downloaded three non-overlapping 40 MiB ranges at 0, 400 MiB, and 800 MiB from one RARD log shard.
  2. Parsed 1,023,019 impressions across 102,311 slates and aggregated clicks by rank.
  3. Checked strategy metadata; `algorithm-class` was missing in all sampled rows, so no algorithm-arm lift is claimed.
  4. Saved aggregate metrics only. The raw source rows are not included with this portfolio.

Limits: This is a byte-range sample, not a random sample or the full dataset. RARD concerns scholarly articles, not short-form video. The logs lack creator, content-quality, originality, moderation, and negative-feedback labels. Clicks are a weak value proxy, and position is confounded with item quality.
